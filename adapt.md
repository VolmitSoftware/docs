Adapt adds passive skills and abilities without the power creep of a typical RPG plugin. The
design goal is quality-of-life enhancement on top of vanilla, not replacement of it.

**Root command:** `/adapt`
**Player entry point:** right-click any **bookshelf face**
**Soft dependencies:** PlaceholderAPI, WorldGuard, Factions, ChestProtect, Residence

## How it works

Players open the Adapt GUI by right-clicking a bookshelf. Skills level as the player performs
the matching activity — mining raises Pickaxe, swimming raises Seaborne — and levelling
awards knowledge, which is spent on individual *adaptations* within that skill.

Every skill and every adaptation can be configured or disabled independently.

## Skill lines

Adapt ships 21 skill lines:

Agility · Architect · Axes · Blocking · Brewing · Chronos · Crafting · Discovery · Enchanting ·
Excavation · Herbalism · Hunter · Nether · Pickaxe · Ranged · Rift · Seaborne · Swords ·
Taming · Tragoul · Unarmed

Each has between three and thirteen adaptations. The full list is in the
[Blacklist Reference](/adapt/blacklist), which is also the canonical list of what exists.
