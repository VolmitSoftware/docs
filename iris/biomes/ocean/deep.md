`ocean/deep` is a direct sea root in the Overworld and Underworld packs. The packs preserve its spatial identity while changing materials, registered biome effects and ecology for their respective dimensions.

## Selection role and weight

Biome and region `rarity` values contribute relative weight as `1 / rarity`; slot fractions below show this key's entries in each region role list, not a whole-world probability.

## Terrain and generator

The terrain generators use `surfaceDetail: 0.5` to smooth small surface variations. See [Generators and noise](/iris/14-generators-noise).

`smooth-dunes` maps to `-80..-40` relative to the dimension fluid height.

## Overworld treatment

Derivative `minecraft:deep_ocean`; vanilla derivative `minecraft:deep_ocean`. Primary sea palette: `minecraft:sand`, `minecraft:clay`, `minecraft:gravel`, `minecraft:dirt`. Decorator blocks: `minecraft:kelp_plant`, `minecraft:seagrass`, `minecraft:tall_seagrass`.

## Underworld treatment

Derivative `minecraft:basalt_deltas`; vanilla derivative `minecraft:basalt_deltas`. Primary sea palette: `minecraft:blackstone`, `minecraft:gravel`. Decorator blocks: `minecraft:lava`. Entity spawners: `nether/lava`, `nether/cave`. Custom registered biome id: `underworld_ocean_deep_a70934a1`.

## Child and floating variants

No child or floating-child biome is declared by this root in either pack.

## Inspect it in game

Run these from a world generated by the relevant pack:

```text
/iris find biome ocean/deep
/iris what biome
/iris edit biome ocean/deep
```

`find` locates the biome and normally teleports on Bukkit; `what biome` confirms the biome at the current position. Cave roots may require descending into the carved volume after `find`. `edit` opens the active pack JSON on Bukkit; on modded, use the current-biome form where appropriate.
