## Inspect it in game

Run these from a world generated by the relevant pinned pack:

```text
/iris find biome carving/drip-lite
/iris what biome
/iris edit biome carving/drip-lite
```

`find` locates the biome and normally teleports on Bukkit; `what biome` confirms the biome at the current position. Cave roots may require descending into the carved volume after `find`. `edit` opens the active pack JSON on Bukkit; on modded, use the current-biome form where appropriate.
