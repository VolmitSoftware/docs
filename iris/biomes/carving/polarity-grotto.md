`carving/polarity-grotto` is a direct cave root in the Overworld and Underworld packs. The packs preserve its spatial identity while changing materials, registered biome effects and ecology for their respective dimensions.

Both packs disable standalone cave aquifers in this family and its children. Contained hydrology uses water in Overworld and lava in Underworld. The profiles retain their separate deep-lava permission.

## Inspect it in game

Run these from a world generated by the relevant pack:

```text
/iris find biome carving/polarity-grotto
/iris what biome
/iris edit biome carving/polarity-grotto
```

`find` locates the biome and normally teleports on Bukkit; `what biome` confirms the biome at the current position. Cave roots may require descending into the carved volume after `find`. `edit` opens the active pack JSON on Bukkit; on modded, use the current-biome form where appropriate.
