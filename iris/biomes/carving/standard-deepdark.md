`carving/standard-deepdark` is not selected from any region's `caveBiomes` list. Both pinned dimensions add it through the enabled dimension-level `global-deepdark-band`, which contributes an extra carving pass throughout an absolute world-Y band.

## Dimension-level role

| Pack | Entry id | Absolute world Y | Selection weight |
|------|----------|------------------|------------------|
| Overworld 4002 | `global-deepdark-band` | `-250..-175` | Not region-weighted; enabled across the dimension band |
| Underworld 1005 | `global-deepdark-band` | `-250..-175` | Not region-weighted; enabled across the dimension band |

Because this is a dimension carving entry, every enabled entry whose biome has an enabled cave profile adds that profile as a band-restricted pass on top of normal blended cave profiles.

## Carving profile

**Overworld.** Enabled local cave profile with declared vertical range `6..81`; sample step `2`; surface clearance `14`; surface breaking `disabled`; fluids `disabled`; lava `disabled`; a minimum depth below surface of `60`.

**Underworld.** Enabled local cave profile with declared vertical range `6..81`; sample step `2`; surface clearance `14`; surface breaking `disabled`; fluids `disabled`; lava `disabled`; a minimum depth below surface of `60`.

## Inspect it in game

Run these from a world generated by the relevant pinned pack:

```text
/iris find biome carving/standard-deepdark
/iris what biome
/iris edit biome carving/standard-deepdark
```

`find` locates the biome and normally teleports on Bukkit; `what biome` confirms the biome at the current position. Cave roots may require descending into the carved volume after `find`. `edit` opens the active pack JSON on Bukkit; on modded, use the current-biome form where appropriate.
