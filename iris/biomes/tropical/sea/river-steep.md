`tropical/sea/river-steep` is hydrology channel content in the Overworld 4007 and Underworld 1010 packs. The packs preserve its spatial identity while changing materials, registered biome effects and ecology for their respective dimensions.

## Selection role

The tropical region and `tropical/mountain` list this biome in `riverPolicy.surfaceBiomes`, alongside the other tropical river variant. It supplies content to accepted channels and is absent from the region’s ordinary sea list. Its transit-only policy allows a course through it but starts no sources or outlets.

## Terrain and generator

`mountain` maps to `-32..-10` relative to the dimension fluid height.

Hydrology supplies the accepted channel height and shape when selecting this content biome.

## Overworld 4007 treatment

Derivative `minecraft:warm_ocean`; vanilla derivative `minecraft:warm_ocean`. Primary sea palette: `minecraft:sand`, `minecraft:clay`, `minecraft:gravel`, `minecraft:dirt`. Decorator blocks: `minecraft:kelp_plant`, `minecraft:seagrass`, `minecraft:tall_seagrass`. Object set: `clutter/ellipsoid1`.

## Underworld 1010 treatment

Derivative `minecraft:basalt_deltas`; vanilla derivative `minecraft:basalt_deltas`. Primary sea palette: `minecraft:blackstone`, `minecraft:gravel`. Decorator blocks: `minecraft:lava`. Object set: `underworld/basalt/clutter/ellipsoid1`. Entity spawners: `nether/lava`, `nether/cave`. Custom registered biome id: `underworld_tropical_sea_river_steep_1fe60d83`.

## Child and floating variants

No child or floating-child biome is declared by this root in either pinned pack.

## Inspect it in game

Run these from a world generated by the relevant pinned pack:

```text
/iris find biome tropical/sea/river-steep
/iris what biome
/iris edit biome tropical/sea/river-steep
```

`find` locates the biome and normally teleports on Bukkit; `what biome` confirms the biome at the current position. Cave roots may require descending into the carved volume after `find`. `edit` opens the active pack JSON on Bukkit; on modded, use the current-biome form where appropriate.
